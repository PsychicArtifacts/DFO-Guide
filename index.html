A field guide to the numbers Daggerfall Online doesn't show you · for [ROSE]

The game never says a shop is quality 19 or a dungeon ranks 21. It gives you hints instead: a shop that greets you with incense and soft music, a dungeon called a Coven on the travel map.

This guide turns those hints into numbers you can use. Set your Luck, level and Renown on the right, and every section below updates to match your character.

Section 1

Shops

Every shop tells you its quality in the first line it says when you walk in. There are five tiers. Buy plain goods in the bad shops and sell your loot in the best ones.

What did the shop say when you walked in?

  • Shops count your Luck in whole tens. At 69 you shop like 60, so one more point can be a full step up.
  • Only pawn shops sell magic items, and only from the middle of the Average tier up.
  • Gem stores need the Good tier for emeralds, and a bit higher for diamonds and sapphires.
  • Alchemists keep rare ingredients in better shops: Mummy Wrappings, Gryphon Feather and Nymph Hair need the Good tier, Holy Relic needs Best, and Dragon's Scales only the very top of Best. Every alchemist stocks Unicorn Horn, Basilisk Eye and Pearl.
  • Repairing an item doesn't raise what a shop pays for it online. Shops pay for the condition it was in when you found it.
  • Better shops watch their shelves. A shop's quality adds directly to your chance of getting caught stealing.
Section 2

Dungeons

The travel map shows each dungeon's type, and the type decides who lives there, what metal drops and how much magic loot you'll find. Metal and magic are ranked separately, so the best place for one isn't always the best for the other.

What are you after?

Chance a foe carries a top-metal piece, at your Luck

  • Metal and magic are separate rankings. A Giant Stronghold drops weak metal but better magic than a Prison.
  • Human Strongholds, Prisons and Ruined Castles scale their metal with your character level, up to a Vampire Haunt's.
  • Mines, caves and nests sit at the bottom for metal, however hard they fight.
  • Best farming: Desecrated Temples have the most daedra. Covens have the best metal plus four kinds of daedra. Bring mithril or better.
Section 3

Weapon Metals

Ten metals, from iron to daedric. Some foes need a minimum metal. Online, a weaker weapon still hits them, but it does 20% less damage for each tier you're short, down to a minimum of 10%.

What is your weapon made of?

  • Silver works like steel against most foes, but does full damage to every ghost, wraith, vampire and werecreature. Carry one early.
  • Fists and spells ignore metal requirements completely.
  • Silver and adamantium hold more enchantment than mithril.
  • Leather armour is 3 and chain is 6. Shields ignore metal: buckler 1, round 2, kite 3, tower 4.
Section 4

Loot Rarity

Seven rarity tiers, and only the first four drop from enemies. What you kill and where you kill it set the odds. Luck nudges them up or down.

The last three never drop at random: Aetheric pieces are a boss's own set, Artifacts are Daggerfall's quest rewards, and Gilded is a single item, the Hourlock.

What did you just kill?

  • Bad-luck protection is real. Every 25 pieces you pick up below Legendary raise your Legendary odds by a fifth, up to triple. Getting one resets it.
  • Rare pieces drop unidentified, and their bonuses already work before you identify them.
  • Salvaging breaks a piece into Welkynd Shards: Magic 1, Rare 3, Legendary 8, Exalted 15. Each hone on a piece doubles the next hone's price.
  • Resistance lines on gear stop at 45% per element. Race and spells stack on top of that.
Section 5

Guild Ranks

Every guild has ten ranks, and every promotion checks the same four things.

Which rank are you looking at?

5
  • The fastest way to Daedra summoning is the Mages Guild or the Temple of Julianos, both at rank 6.
  • A knightly order's Seneschal gives you armour in the metal matching your rank, and you keep one piece. Rank 5 is mithril, the cheapest way to get it.
  • Stendarr gives a rank-in-50 chance to survive a killing blow. At rank 9 that's almost one death in five ignored.
  • The Thieves Guild never kicks you out. The Fighters Guild pays 10% more for quests and charges 10% less for repairs with every rank.
  • Online, rank 8 and 9 are council chairs, won on merit at each Sunday's Turning. Reputation arrives at most 15 a real day.
Section 6

Enchanting

Every item has a fixed amount of enchantment room. Powers use up room. Side effects and trapped souls add room back.

Pick an item, then add enchantments.

  • One Daedra Lord's soul adds 8,000 room, more than three amulets' worth.
  • Up to 10 lines total, side effects and souls included. Costs 10 gold for every point of powers. Side effects and souls don't lower the gold.
  • The newer armour types, like brigandine, can hold less than these numbers. Check the total the enchanter shows you.
  • Cast When Held spells wear the item a little as long as it's worn. Pieces with Repairs Objects now mend everything you wear by 2% a tick.
  • Spell strength follows character level. Spell cost follows your skill in its school: (110 − skill) ÷ 400 of its gold price, never under 5. Range costs ×1.5, an area ×2, an area at range ×2.5.
Section 7

Level & Renown

You have two levels. Character level comes from your skills and decides what spawns, how hard your spells hit and how much gold corpses carry. Renown is the online level, kept on the server: each point adds 3 health and 2 magicka.

What the game spawns at your level

Your Renown

Party in the room

Regular

Foes as Daggerfall made them. One dungeon in five has a single elite. Respawns in 20 real minutes.

Elite

×2 health, ×2 damage. Three or four elites at ×7 health and ×4 damage. Respawns in 40 minutes.

Abyss

Foes level 24 or more at ×4 health and ×2.5 damage, elites ×9 and ×4.5. Appears 2 to 4 squares from the main city of the busiest region, lasts 48 real hours unless beaten, and collapses 3 minutes after it falls. The next one appears 2 hours later.

Rewards for every fighter: 400 gold per level, a piece that's Legendary one time in four (otherwise Rare), two more Rare or better, a Brass of Numidium piece one time in three, and the Hourlock one time in fifty.

  • Loot is rolled using the host's Renown. In a group, the player with the highest Renown should host the dungeon.
  • Class foes (mages, knights, thieves) match your character level, so they give less Renown than a monster in the same fight.
  • An account earns at most 15,000 Renown an hour. Raids pay three quests' worth; Sethrakul pays six.
  • Open-world elites are one foe in twenty from level 3: two Magic pieces, a 6% Legendary chance and 20 to 60 gold per level.
  • Champions (Mighty, Stalwart, Swift, Vampiric, Thorned) have double health. A Stalwart has triple. They drop better loot.
Section 8

Calendar & Holidays

Online, a game day lasts 2 real hours and a game year turns every 30 real days. Money holidays happen for everyone at once. Times below are in your own time zone.

  • A festival's half price doesn't stack with the online half price on potions, and shops still pay you at most half of full price. You can't flip festival buys for profit.
  • Online, the summoner calls whichever Daedric prince you name, on any day, for 200,000 gold minus 1,000 for every point of their regard. The Glenmoril witches always call Hircine.
  • Months in order: Morning Star, Sun's Dawn, First Seed, Rain's Hand, Second Seed, Midyear, Sun's Height, Last Seed, Hearthfire, Frostfall, Sun's Dusk, Evening Star.
